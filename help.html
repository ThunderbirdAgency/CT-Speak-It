Mockingbird by Thunderbird

A little help for your bird.

Download availability

Download buttons activate when signed, verified installers are available. If a button says “preparing for launch,” the installer has not been released yet. You can use the web workspace once account access is enabled.

The shortcut does nothing

Make sure Mockingbird is running in your menu bar or system tray. Open Settings and choose another shortcut if another app already uses it. On Mac, allow Microphone access, and Accessibility access for automatic pasting.

My words did not appear

Click a normal editable field. Secure fields and some remote apps may reject paste automation. Your output is available in local history if history is enabled, and on your clipboard. Use Cmd/Ctrl+V to paste it yourself.

Can I recover a failed recording?

On desktop, use Retry last recording in Settings while the app is still open. The recording is held in memory until another recording replaces it or you quit. Audio is not saved to disk. Cleanup failures preserve the raw transcript.

My connection code expired

Create another code in My devices. Codes expire after 10 minutes and only work once. A connected desktop session lasts 30 days. Revoke a lost device from your account.

What are the usage limits?

Pro includes up to 240 recordings and 500 text requests per day, with three-minute recordings in the app. The limits reset at midnight UTC. A failed provider request can still count toward the limit.

Gifts and billing

A gift activates Pro for the period shown in your account without a card. It never turns into a paid subscription automatically. Existing paid subscriptions continue until you cancel renewal through Manage billing, even if you redeem a gift.

Get in touch

Open your Mockingbird account. If support contact details are not available yet, contact the Thunderbird team that invited you. Do not include client transcripts, passwords or payment card details in support requests.
