Mockingbird Pro · Product terms · September 5, 2026

What Pro includes

Pro provides cloud dictation, cleanup, rewrites, saved responses and optional writing memory on supported clients. Desktop automatic paste depends on operating-system permissions and the target application. The web workspace supports recording and copying in compatible browsers. Native mobile keyboards, CRM execution, meeting recording and wake-phrase activation are not part of this release.

Membership and gifts

Paid Pro is $15 USD per month and renews until cancelled through the billing portal. Checkout shows the final amount before you subscribe. A gift grants the access period shown on redemption, requires no card, and does not automatically renew into paid access. Gift codes may have a claim deadline and a limited number of recipients. Existing paid subscriptions are not automatically cancelled by a gift.

Usage

Pro allows 240 recordings and 500 text requests per UTC day, with additional limits on account actions to protect the service. Do not share account credentials or use automated bulk requests. We may restrict abusive access. Internet and supported audio hardware are required.

Review your work

Speech recognition and generated rewrites can make mistakes. Check names, dates, prices and other details before sending or relying on output. You remain responsible for your communications and for having permission to process the information you submit.

Support and cancellation

You can manage renewal in your account under Pro & gifts. Contact Thunderbird through Help for billing issues, account requests or support. Any rights that apply to you under applicable law remain in effect.
