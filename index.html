How it works

One key. That's the whole interface.

  1. Press and talk

    Put your cursor anywhere and hold the shortcut. A small pill shows it's listening.

  2. It cleans you up

    “Um” disappears. Correct yourself mid-sentence — “2 o'clock, no wait, 3” — and only the 3 survives. Spell an email out loud and it assembles it.

  3. It lands where you were

    Polished text, at your cursor, in whatever app you were already using.

  4. Or it acts

    If what you said was an instruction, you get a card showing exactly what will happen. Press Enter and it's done.

It sounds like you, not like an AI

Mockingbird learns how you write from your own dictations — your phrasing, your sign-off, the way you close a message.

It never loses your words

Formatter down? You get the raw transcript. No field focused? It goes to your clipboard. Every failure still ends with your words somewhere.

No recordings kept

Your audio is transcribed and discarded — Mockingbird never stores a recording. It runs on your team's own deployment, not a shared service.

It gets better every week

It learns your people, your places, your habits.

Week one it fixes filler words. Week four it spells your neighbourhoods right, knows that “the Chens” means the contact in your CRM, and writes your follow-ups the way you write them.

Your vocabulary

Street names, brokerages, client names — the proper nouns every recognizer gets wrong, learned from your own corrections.

Your voice

Sentence length, formality, how you open and close. The polish keeps your voice instead of flattening it.

And you can read all of it

Everything it has picked up is on one page in the app, in plain language. Erase it in one click, or switch learning off entirely.

Questions

How is this different from other dictation apps?

They stop at typing. Mockingbird types just as well, and then does the thing you asked for — creating the contact, logging the call, booking the showing, setting the follow-up — in the system you actually run your business on.

Can it do something I didn't mean?

It shows you every action before running it, with each field filled in, and waits for you to press Enter. Anything ambiguous is treated as ordinary dictation instead — words in the wrong email are a nuisance, a record created by accident is worse.

Which apps does it work in?

All of them. It pastes at your cursor like a keyboard would, so anything you can type into, you can talk into — including apps that have never heard of us.

What does it connect to?

Follow Up Boss works out of the box. Any other product can be connected by describing its endpoints once — no code — and it becomes something you can say.

Is my dictation private?

It runs on your team's own deployment, not a shared service. Audio is never stored. Transcripts are kept so it can learn how you write — that's readable and erasable by you, and can be turned off.

What do I need to install?

One app, and about two minutes: install it, paste the address your team gives you, allow the microphone. There's a setup page with the exact steps.
