A few things you might ask.

Will it type in every app?

The desktop app sends text through the system clipboard into ordinary editable fields. Some secure fields, remote desktops, or apps may block automatic pasting. You can copy your result from local history or use clipboard-only mode. A browser workspace cannot type into other apps on your phone.

Is it always listening?

No. Recording begins when you activate it and stops when you finish or cancel. The “Is that a mockingbird?” wake phrase is a future option; it is not enabled in this release.

Does it send messages or change my CRM?

This release focuses on dictation and rewriting. You review and send your own messages. Direct CRM and calendar actions are being developed separately.

What happens when my gift ends?

You keep your account and can export or erase your saved preferences. To continue Pro, you can redeem another valid gift or choose a paid membership. A gift never starts billing on its own.

Can I use it offline?

Cloud dictation and rewrites need internet access. If cleanup fails after transcription succeeds, the desktop app preserves the raw words. If transcription fails, you can retry the recording during that app session.
