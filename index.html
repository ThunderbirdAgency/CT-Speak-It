Set it up — about a minute

  1. Install it

    Mac: open the .dmg and drag Mockingbird to Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open.
    Windows: run the installer and click through the SmartScreen warning.

  2. Paste the address above, and your name

    Mockingbird opens asking for two things. That's the whole setup — press Save.

  3. Allow the microphone

    It asks the first time you speak. On a Mac, also switch Mockingbird on under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, so text pastes itself. Until you do, your words wait on the clipboard and you press Cmd+V.

Using it

Ctrl+Shift+Space  Dictate. Put your cursor anywhere — an email, a text box, a CRM field — press it, talk, press it again. Your words land polished. Escape cancels.

Ctrl+Shift+K  Tell it to do something. “Add Maria Lopez, 555-0142, from the open house, and remind me to call her Monday.” It shows you exactly what it's about to do — press Enter and it's done. Nothing gets written until you press it.
