Product data practices · September 5, 2026

What is processed

When you activate recording, audio is sent to the configured transcription provider (Groq, Deepgram, or OpenAI). Transcribed text and rewrite requests are sent to Anthropic for cleanup and rewriting. These services process requests under the operator’s provider agreements and retention settings. Mockingbird does not promise that processing never leaves your device or that providers retain nothing.

What Mockingbird saves

Your account stores your membership, gift redemption, connected devices, usage counts, and the writing preferences and saved responses you choose to save. The consumer service does not save raw audio or a server archive of dictation transcripts. Infrastructure may keep ordinary request and error metadata. Payment records are managed through Stripe, and sign-in through Clerk.

Optional memory

Memory starts off. You can enable it, see it, edit it, export it, or erase it in your account. A sample submitted for suggestions is processed for that request; the suggestions are not saved until you approve them by saving the form. Avoid including private client or deal details in samples. Memory is not used to build hidden profiles of clients or for advertising.

On your device

Desktop history is off by default. If you enable it, the app stores up to 200 recent results locally; you can clear them in Settings. Dictated output is copied to the system clipboard, which may be visible to clipboard managers or system clipboard syncing. A failed recording can remain in app memory for retry until replaced or the app closes. A browser draft exists only in the open page.

Your choices

Turn memory off without losing the preferences you saved, or erase it to remove those preferences and saved responses. Erasing also attempts to remove older server transcript/profile records associated with your verified account ID. Previous installations that used a manually entered name may need the Thunderbird team to help locate older records. Clearing cloud memory does not clear local desktop history.

Account records

Gift and billing records remain separate from memory so access and payments can be administered. Use your account’s sign-in profile controls to manage your identity and contact Thunderbird through Help for account-record deletion requests. Cancelling a membership does not erase your writing preferences automatically.

No background listening

The app does not continuously record and does not enable a wake phrase. Only record information you are entitled to share with the service, and review output before sending it to others.
