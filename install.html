Your first minute with Mockingbird.

  1. Sign in to your account and activate Pro or redeem a gift code.
  2. Download and open the desktop app.
  3. In your account’s My devices tab, create a connection code. Paste it into the app.
  4. Click into an email or document. Press Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Space, speak, then press again.
  5. For rewrites, select text and press Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R. Say your instruction and review before applying.

On your phone?

Use the web workspace to record, edit and copy text. It does not replace your phone’s keyboard across other apps. Native iPhone and Android keyboards are a separate release.
